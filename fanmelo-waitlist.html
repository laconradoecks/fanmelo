Questions

Good to know

When does FANMELO open?

We're opening in waves, country by country, so payments, payouts and ID checks work properly where you live. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day your spot opens.

What does it cost creators?

Creating a page is free. A small fee is added on top of what fans give, so creators receive the full amount fans chip in.

What can I list as a need?

Products like a camera or laptop, bills like rent or studio time (with the provider's invoice), or goals. Every need needs a reason. Adult items and anything offered in return for money aren't allowed.

How do fans know their money is used well?

Product prices are checked against real stores, creators are ID-verified, and every funded need needs proof of purchase. Payments are handled by licensed payment companies, and needs that aren't bought can be reported for a refund.
