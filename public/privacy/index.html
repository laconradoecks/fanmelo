Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

FANMELO isn't open yet. Right now the only personal information we collect is what you type into the waitlist form. This page explains what that is, why we need it and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

FANMELO is a Yosaki company. When this page says "we" or "us", it means FANMELO. You can reach us about anything on this page at privacy@fanmelo.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Whether you joined as a creator or a fan
  • Your TikTok, Instagram or YouTube handle, if you chose to add one
  • Your country
  • The date and time you joined

We don't ask for your name, ID or payment details on the waitlist. The site doesn't use advertising or analytics trackers and doesn't set cookies. The form suggests a country based on your device's time zone, but that guess stays in your browser; we only receive the country you submit.

Why we collect it

  • To email you when FANMELO opens where you live. We're opening country by country, so we need your country to know when your spot is ready.
  • To give creators their page link first, and to tell fans when creators go live. That's why we ask whether you're a creator or a fan.
  • Your handle helps us prepare your creator page and check it's really you when you sign up properly.

We only email you about FANMELO access. We use your details because you gave them to us for this purpose, and you can withdraw that at any time (see "Your choices" below).

Who can see it

Only the FANMELO team, through a password-protected admin page. We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't share them with advertisers.

The waitlist is stored on a server we rent from DigitalOcean, a hosting company, in the United States. DigitalOcean stores the data for us but doesn't use it for anything else. If we start using another service to send waitlist emails, we'll list it here.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist details until FANMELO opens in your country and we've invited you, or until you ask us to remove them, whichever comes first. If you create a FANMELO account later, that account will have its own privacy terms, which we'll show you before you sign up.

Keeping it safe

The site only works over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, the admin page is password-protected, and the waitlist isn't reachable from the internet in any other way.

Your choices

Email privacy@fanmelo.com from the address you signed up with and we'll do any of these within 30 days:

  • See what we hold about you
  • Correct it, for example a wrong handle or country
  • Remove you from the waitlist

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Uganda that's the Personal Data Protection Office.

Children

The waitlist is for people aged 18 and over. If you think someone younger has signed up, tell us and we'll remove them.

Changes to this page

If what we collect or why changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll email people on the waitlist before it takes effect.
